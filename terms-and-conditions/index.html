Terms & conditions

Last updated October 2026

These terms apply to all trips arranged by KD Global Commerce & Services, Thimphu. Your booking confirmation gives the details for your trip.

Booking & payment

Your booking is confirmed once we receive your deposit. Full payment is due before we apply for your visa.

Prices

Quotes include the visa fee and SDF at current government rates. If these change, the price may change.

Cancellations

Cancel in writing. Charges are listed in your booking confirmation. The visa fee is non-refundable.

Changes

Weather, roads or flights may change plans. We'll offer the best alternative.

Insurance

Travel insurance for your whole stay is required, including altitude cover for treks.

Liability

We're not liable for events beyond our control, such as weather, illness or flight disruption.

Privacy

Your details are used only to arrange your trip.

Law

These terms are governed by the laws of Bhutan.
